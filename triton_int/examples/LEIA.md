# Example projects (International Edition)

Three projects, one building, three cities. The building is the manual's tutorial house, the same in
all three so that the results compare: 150 m² of roof (runoff coefficient 0.85), 4 residents,
150 L/person.day, 28% of the demand non-potable (toilet flushing and outdoor use), 2 mm first-flush
discard, pressure-booster supply, a volume sweep up to 20,000 L in 1,000 L steps.

| File | City | Currency | Rainfall (`data/rainfall/`) | Tariff valid from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `new_york.triton` | New York | US$ | `NewYork.csv`, 1,125 mm/year | 01/07/2026 |
| `london.triton` | London | £ | `London.csv`, 649 mm/year | 01/04/2026 (inferred) |
| `cape_town.triton` | Cape Town | R (ZAR) | `CapeTown.csv`, 495 mm/year | 01/07/2026 (inferred) |

The projects are generated by `scripts/gerar_exemplos_int.py` (same inputs, same files) and checked by
`tests/unit/test_exemplos_da_edicao.py`, which recomputes each bill by hand from the numbers below.
The reports of the three examples (executive, environmental/ESG, technical dossier; PDF, in English)
are in `reports/exemplos/<city>/`, made by `scripts/gerar_relatorios_exemplo.py`.

**Costs are not set.** Costs of works, pump, treatment and maintenance, and the electricity tariff,
are left at zero: they depend on the site and on the market, and no official source was used for
them. Enter your own in stage 5. Until you do, the economic analysis shows only the saving on the
water bill (payback and NPV then say little). Each project carries this notice in its `analysis`
block. Rainfall is ERA5 *reanalysis* (grid cell of about 9 km, see `data/rainfall/LEIA.md`), not a rain
gauge record: use a measured series for a real design.

**Not set, on purpose:** no local CAPEX or OPEX exists for these examples, and none was invented: cistern, pump,
piping, labour, maintenance and chlorine costs are zero, and the electricity tariff is zero. The grid emission
factor has no local value either: the project does not set one (the program's starting value applies until you enter
yours). Payback and NPV shown for these projects are therefore **not results**; the tests assert only the potable-water
saving and the water bill.

## Tariffs (collected on 14/10/2026)

**Status: illustrative.** These tariffs are published prices transcribed so that the examples run and the tests have bills to recompute. They are **not** a verified tariff database: the document URL and SHA-256 were not recorded at collection, so none of the figures below can be traced to a stored copy. [A COMPLETAR: the maintainer is to add, for each utility, the document URL, the access date and the SHA-256 of the file.] Do not use them for a real project; enter the tariff of your own utility.

The figures were read from the official tariff documents of each utility and entered as printed.
Document URL and SHA-256 hash: **not recorded** (they were not supplied when the examples were built;
to be added by the maintainer next to each source below).

### New York

- Source: NYC Water Board and NYC Department of Environmental Protection (DEP), Water and Sewer Rates, FY2027.
- In force: from 1 July 2026.
- Water US$ 5.35 per CCF (1 CCF = 100 ft³ = 2.8316847 m³, so US$ 1.8894 per m³); sewer 159% of the water charge;
  minimum charge US$ 0.49 per day.
- Entered as: water, one open step at US$ 5.35 / 2.8316847 per m³; sewer = 159% of the water parcel; the minimum
  charge as a monthly floor on the water parcel, US$ 0.49 × 30.4375 = US$ 14.91 per month (the engine's month).
- Assumption to confirm: whether the minimum charge applies to water alone or to water and sewer together
  (it does not bind at the example's consumption, 18 m³ per month).

### London

- Source: Thames Water, charges 2026-27, metered household.
- In force: charging year 2026-27, taken to start on 1 April 2026 (inferred; confirm).
- Water £ 2.7346 per m³; wastewater £ 1.4721 per m³; standing charges £ 66.87 per year (water) and £ 128.13 per year
  (wastewater; £ 80.43 with the surface-water drainage rebate). No VAT on household water and sewerage.
- Entered as: water standing charge £ 66.87 / 12 per month and £ 2.7346 per m³; wastewater £ 1.4721 per m³ plus
  £ 128.13 / 12 per month as a fixed amount (the standing charges cancel in the saving).
- Assumptions to confirm: the £ 80.43 rebate case is not applied; wastewater is charged on 100% of the metered
  volume (no return-to-sewer allowance was supplied).

### Cape Town

- Source: City of Cape Town, Tariffs 2026/27, domestic, non-indigent, no water restrictions; values include VAT; R per kl (1 kl = 1 m³).
- In force: tariff year 2026/27, taken to start on 1 July 2026 (inferred; confirm).
- Water: 0-6 kl 25.05; 6-10.5 kl 34.42; 10.5-35 kl 54.60; above 35 kl 109.98; monthly standing charge R 103.65 (domestic 12–20 mm, with VAT).
- Sanitation, on 70% of the water consumed, up to 35 kl: 0-4.2 kl 18.28; 4.2-7.35 kl 25.11; 7.35-24.5 kl 42.18; 24.5-35 kl 73.91; monthly standing charge R 56.66 (domestic 12–20 mm, with VAT).
- Entered as: water standing charge R 103.65 per month and volumetric steps; sanitation by steps on 0.7 × the water volume plus R 56.66 per month as a fixed amount (the standing charges cancel in the saving).
- Assumptions to confirm: above 35 kl of sanitation volume (50 kl of water) nothing more is charged.
