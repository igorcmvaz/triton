WELCOME TO THE TRITON INSTALLER — INTERNATIONAL EDITION

ABOUT THE SOFTWARE:
Triton simulates rainwater harvesting systems in buildings. From the local
rainfall, the roof catchment and the building's water demand, it sizes the
tank and assesses water performance, financial return, carbon impact and
certification scoring. This edition is region-neutral: you supply the
rainfall series, the tariffs and the costs, in the currency of your choice.
